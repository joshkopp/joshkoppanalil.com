JKMLWord Bomb

A party word game for 1–16 friends. When the bomb is on you, type a word that contains the letters before it blows up.
How to play: When the arrow points at you, type a word containing the letters on the bomb (like ING → SINGER) and press Enter. The fuse is secret, so be quick. If it blows up on you, you lose a heart. Words can't be reused. Use every letter in your bonus row to earn a heart back. The last player standing wins.

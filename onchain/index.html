roll = HMAC-SHA256(seed, playerSeed:rollNumber), first 32 bits scaled to 0.00 to 99.99. Fingerprint is SHA-256(seed). House edge 1%.

Built for the University Blockchain Conference 2026 hackathon by Josh Koppanalil. Solana devnet and play chips only. Nothing here has value and nothing here is a wager.

The house runs on a server and posts every fingerprint to devnet from its own wallet. Your browser never trusts it: each revealed seed is checked against its fingerprint before the roll counts. If the house is unreachable the game falls back to a house in your browser and says so. Use Put on devnet on any stub to sign the result into the chain yourself.

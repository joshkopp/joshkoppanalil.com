How Monyx works

Monyx lets you send money to anyone using their @username, the way you'd text a friend. This page explains every part of it in plain language. You don't need to know anything about crypto.

Pay by nameType @maya, pick an amount, done. No bank details.
Split anythingDinner, rent, tickets. Everyone gets a request for their share.
Buy cryptoStart with $1. Buy, sell or swap nine coins at live prices.

01The basics

Monyx is built on Solana, a public payment network that anyone can use, like the internet is for email. When you pay someone, the money moves from your account to theirs over that network in about a second.

You never see any of that. Monyx turns long account numbers into usernames, shows everything in dollars, and handles the technical parts for you.

The one thing to remember: your money lives in your account, which only you control. Monyx can't move it, freeze it, or spend it. That's also why your recovery key matters so much (more on that below).

02Your account

Signing up

Signing up takes three steps: your name, an @username, and saving your recovery key. There's no password and no email.

What actually happens

When you sign up, your browser creates a private key: a long secret code that works like the only key to a safe. It never leaves your device. From it comes your account number, which is public, like a mailbox address. Monyx saves just three things: your name, your username, and which account number belongs to it.

Your recovery key

Your recovery key is that private key, written out so you can save it. You need it to log in on a new phone or after clearing your browser.

Monyx can't reset it. There's no "forgot password" because we never had it. If you lose your recovery key and your browser data, the account can't be recovered. And anyone who has it can spend your balance, so never share it, not even with someone claiming to be Monyx.

Logging in

To log in, your device proves it holds the key by signing a short message ("Sign in to Monyx", plus the time). Monyx checks the signature and lets you in for 30 days. The key itself is never sent anywhere. If you already use a Phantom crypto wallet, you can log in with that instead.

03Paying & requesting

Tap the green + button, type an amount, choose who it's for, and add a note if you want.

Monyx isn't in the middle of the payment. It goes straight from your account to theirs, which is why it's fast and why Monyx doesn't charge a fee.

Requests

Tap Request instead of Pay and they'll see it on their home screen with Pay and Decline buttons. When they pay, Monyx checks the network to confirm the money really arrived, then marks the request paid. You can cancel a request you sent any time.

Payments are final

Like handing someone cash, a payment can't be reversed once it's sent. If you pay the wrong person, ask them to send it back.

04Splitting a bill

On the pay screen, type the total and tap Split a bill with friends. Add people and choose whether to include yourself, and Monyx works out each share.

Say dinner was $60 for three people including you. Each share is $20, so your two friends each get a request for $20 and you collect $40.

05Adding money

New accounts get a welcome bonus to try things out. After that, tap Add money on the home screen:

  • Get free test money: copy your account number, open the free test-money site, and paste it in. It shows up within a minute.
  • Get paid by a friend: share your pay link or QR code.

06Buying crypto

The Invest tab has nine popular coins: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USD Coin, XRP, Dogecoin, Cardano, Chainlink, Avalanche and Litecoin. Prices are live and update every minute.

ActionWhat happens
BuyYou pay from your Monyx cash, and Monyx adds the coin to your account at the live price. The smallest buy is $1.
SellMonyx takes the coin back and pays the cash into your balance.
SwapTrade one coin straight for another at today's prices. Instant, no fee.

Coin prices go up and down, so the dollar value of what you own changes over time. The Invest tab shows how much it moved today.

How it differs from payments: your cash sits in your own account, but the coins you buy are recorded and kept by Monyx, the way a brokerage app holds stock for you. That's what makes buying and swapping instant.

07Safety & privacy

What Monyx knows

  • Your name, username and account number
  • Payment requests you send and receive
  • Your crypto trades and balances

What Monyx never has

  • Your recovery key or private key
  • A password, email or bank details. We never ask for them.

Payments are public. Solana is a public network, so anyone can look up an account number and see its payments, amounts and notes. They won't see your name unless they already know which account is yours. Keep anything private out of payment notes.

When something goes wrong

If a payment fails, Monyx tells you in plain words: "Nothing was sent," or "We couldn't confirm it yet, check your activity." If a crypto purchase goes through but doesn't get recorded straight away, Monyx remembers it and finishes it the next time you open the app.

08Test mode

Monyx is in test mode right now, which is why you'll see a yellow "Test mode" tag. Everything works for real, but it runs on Solana's test network, so the money is play money with no value. You can't cash it out and you can't lose real money. It's a safe way to try everything.

Your balance is held in Solana's own currency (SOL) and shown in dollars at the live price, so the dollar figure can shift slightly even when you haven't done anything.

09Monyx Pro

For the curious and for developers. Open it from the Me tab.

  • Wallet tracer: enter any account number to see who it's sent money to and received money from, drawn as a map you can click through.
  • API market: data services that software and AI agents can pay for one request at a time, a fraction of a cent each, with no sign-up. The agent asks, gets a price, pays, and gets the answer. Developer instructions are in llms.txt.

10Questions

Does Monyx charge fees?

No. The Solana network charges a tiny fee per payment, a fraction of a cent, and in test mode that's play money too.

I lost my recovery key. Can you help?

If you're still logged in on a device, open the Me tab, tap Recovery key and save it now. If you've lost both the key and your logged-in device, the account can't be recovered. Nobody holds a copy, including us.

Someone asked for my recovery key. Should I give it?

Never. Anyone with it can empty your account. Monyx will never ask for it.

Can I undo a payment?

No, payments are final. Ask the person to send it back.

Why did my balance change when I didn't do anything?

Your balance is held in SOL and shown in dollars, so it moves a little with SOL's price. Crypto you own also changes value as prices move.

Can I use Monyx on more than one device?

Yes. On the new device, choose "I already have an account" and paste your recovery key.

My friend isn't on Monyx. Can I still pay them?

Send them your pay link. They'll see who's paying and can sign up in under a minute. If they already have a Solana account, you can also paste their account number.

Guide

How Monyx works

Monyx is the money app you can talk to. Say “send Maya 20 for pizza” and it's ready to go, or type their @username the way you'd text a friend. This page explains every part of it in plain language. You don't need to know anything about crypto.

Just say it“Send Maya 20 for pizza.” Monyx fills it in, you tap to confirm.
Split anythingDinner, rent, tickets. Everyone gets a request for their share.
Buy cryptoStart with $1. Buy, sell or swap nine coins at live prices.

01The basics

Monyx is built on Solana, a public payment network that anyone can use, like the internet is for email. When you pay someone, the money moves from your account to theirs over that network in about a second.

You never see any of that. Monyx turns long account numbers into usernames, shows everything in dollars, and handles the technical parts for you.

The one thing to remember: your dollars live in your account, which only you control. Monyx can't move, freeze or spend them. (Coins in Invest are the one exception: Monyx holds those for you, like a brokerage. More below.) That's also why your recovery key matters so much.

02Talk to pay

Tap Just say it on the home screen (or the mic on the pay screen) and say what you want, the way you'd tell a friend. Monyx works out who, how much and what for, and shows you the payment, ready to send.

SayWhat happens
“Send Maya 20 for pizza”A $20 payment to Maya with the note 🍕 Pizza, waiting for your tap
“Request 15 from Jordan for coffee”A $15 request to Jordan, waiting for your tap
“Split 60 with Maya and Jordan for dinner”The split screen with both of them added
“What's my balance?”Monyx tells you, out loud
“Who paid me?”Your last few payments, read out loud

Voice never sends money by itself. It only fills in the payment. You always see the person's name, photo and the amount, and nothing moves until you tap Pay. If Monyx mishears, you lose a tap, not your money.

Hearing when you get paid

With Read payments aloud on (in the Me tab), Monyx says it when money arrives: “Maya sent you 14 dollars for pizza.” It also tells you when someone asks you for money. Turn off Monyx talks back if you'd rather read than listen.

This works within a few seconds even if the money came from outside Monyx (another wallet, an exchange). QuickNode Streams watches the network for dollars arriving at your account and tells Monyx straight away.

How it works

Your voice is turned into text by ElevenLabs, a speech company, and Monyx's replies are spoken by an ElevenLabs voice. To help with names, Monyx sends along the names of people you've paid before. Recordings aren't saved by Monyx. If voice isn't switched on, Monyx uses your browser's own speech recognition instead, and you can always type the same sentence.

03Your account

Signing up

Signing up takes three steps: your name, an @username, and saving your recovery key. There's no password and no email.

What actually happens

When you sign up, your browser creates a private key: a long secret code that works like the only key to a safe. It never leaves your device. From it comes your account number, which is public, like a mailbox address. Monyx saves just three things: your name, your username, and which account number belongs to it.

Your recovery key

Your recovery key is that private key, written out so you can save it. You need it to log in on a new phone or after clearing your browser.

Monyx can't reset it. There's no "forgot password" because we never had it. If you lose your recovery key and your browser data, the account can't be recovered. And anyone who has it can spend your balance, so never share it, not even with someone claiming to be Monyx.

Logging in

To log in, your device proves it holds the key by signing a short message ("Sign in to Monyx", plus the time). Monyx checks the signature and lets you in for 30 days. The key itself is never sent anywhere. If you already use a Phantom crypto wallet, you can log in with that instead.

04Paying & requesting

Tap the purple + button, type an amount, choose who it's for, and add a note if you want.

You confirmYour device signs the payment with your key
→
The networkSolana moves the money in about a second
→
They have itIt's in their account, ready to spend

The money goes straight from your account to theirs. Monyx only adds one thing: it pays the network fee (a fraction of a cent) by adding its signature to the payment you already signed. It can't change who the payment goes to or how much. That's why there's no fee for you, and why you never need any crypto to send dollars.

Paying someone who isn't on Monyx

Tap Send with a link, to anyone (or just say "send Mom 20"). Monyx moves the money into a one-off account whose key is only inside the link, and you text the link to them. They open it, make a free account, and the money is theirs. Until then, you can cancel it from Me → Pay links and get it back. Treat the link like cash: anyone who has it can claim it.

Requests

Tap Request instead of Pay and they'll see it on their home screen with Pay and Decline buttons. When they pay, Monyx checks the network to confirm the money really arrived, then marks the request paid. You can cancel a request you sent any time.

Payments are final

Like handing someone cash, a payment can't be reversed once it's sent. If you pay the wrong person, ask them to send it back.

05Splitting a bill

On the pay screen, type the total and tap Split a bill with friends. Add people and choose whether to include yourself, and Monyx works out each share.

Say dinner was $60 for three people including you. Each share is $20, so your two friends each get a request for $20 and you collect $40.

06Adding money

New accounts get $10 in test dollars to try things out. After that, tap Add money on the home screen:

  • Add free test dollars: one tap, $5 a day, straight from Monyx.
  • Get paid by a friend: share your pay link or QR code.
  • Need more for testing? Circle, which issues USDC, runs a free test faucet. The Add money screen links to it.

07Buying crypto

The Invest tab has nine popular coins: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP, Dogecoin, Cardano, Chainlink, Avalanche and Litecoin. Prices are live and update every minute.

ActionWhat happens
BuyYou pay from your Monyx cash, and Monyx adds the coin to your account at the live price. The smallest buy is $1.
SellMonyx takes the coin back and pays the cash into your balance.
SwapTrade one coin straight for another at today's prices. Instant, no fee.

Coin prices go up and down, so the dollar value of what you own changes over time. The Invest tab shows how much it moved today.

Invest works differently from payments. Your dollars sit in your own account, which only you control. Coins you buy are held by Monyx in its own records, the way a brokerage app holds stock for you. When you buy, your dollars go to Monyx; when you sell, Monyx pays dollars back. So for Invest, unlike payments, you're trusting Monyx. In test mode, none of it has real value.

08Safety & privacy

What Monyx knows

  • Your name, username and account number
  • Payment requests you send and receive
  • Your crypto trades and the coins it holds for you
  • Payments it helped send (it pays the fee), and payments it saw arrive for you
  • When you use voice: the sentence you said, long enough to understand it. Recordings aren't kept. Full details are on the privacy page.

What Monyx never has

  • Your recovery key or private key
  • A password, email or bank details. We never ask for them.

Payments are public. Solana is a public network, so anyone can look up an account number and see its payments, amounts and notes. They won't see your name unless they already know which account is yours. Keep anything private out of payment notes.

When something goes wrong

If a payment fails, Monyx tells you in plain words: "Nothing was sent," or "We couldn't confirm it yet, check your activity." If a crypto purchase goes through but doesn't get recorded straight away, Monyx remembers it and finishes it the next time you open the app.

09Test mode

Monyx is in test mode right now, which is why you'll see a "Test mode" tag. The app, the payments and the network are all real software doing real work, but they run on Solana's test network with test dollars (test USDC). Test dollars have no value: you can't cash them out, and you can't lose real money. It's a safe way to try everything.

Your balance is in USDC, a digital dollar, so $1 is always $1. It doesn't move with crypto prices.

10Monyx Pro

For the curious and for developers. Open it from the Me tab.

  • Wallet tracer: enter any account number to see who it's sent money to and received money from, drawn as a map you can click through.
  • API market: data services that software and AI agents can pay for one request at a time, a fraction of a cent each, with no sign-up. The agent asks, gets a price, pays, and gets the answer. Developer instructions are in llms.txt.

11Questions

Does Monyx charge fees?

No. The Solana network charges a fee of a fraction of a cent per payment, and Monyx pays it for you. You never pay a fee or need any crypto to send dollars.

I lost my recovery key. Can you help?

If you're still logged in on a device, open the Me tab, tap Recovery key and save it now. If you've lost both the key and your logged-in device, the account can't be recovered. Nobody holds a copy, including us.

Someone asked for my recovery key. Should I give it?

Never. Anyone with it can empty your account. Monyx will never ask for it.

Can I undo a payment?

No, payments are final. Ask the person to send it back.

Why did my balance change when I didn't do anything?

Your dollar balance only changes when money comes in or goes out. Check Activity: a friend may have paid you. Coins in Invest do change value as prices move.

Can an AI assistant spend my money?

No. If you connect Grok Bot (Me → AI assistant), it can see your activity, ask friends for money and draft payments. Every draft waits on your home screen until you tap Pay. You set a weekly limit and can remove it any time. How it works

Can I use Monyx on more than one device?

Yes. On the new device, choose "I already have an account" and paste your recovery key.

My friend isn't on Monyx. Can I still pay them?

Yes. Send them money with a link: they open it, sign up in under a minute, and it's theirs. You can cancel it until they claim it. If they already have a Solana account, you can also paste their account number.

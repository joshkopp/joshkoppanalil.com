Guide

How Monyx works

Monyx is the money app you can talk to. Say “send Josh 20 for pizza” and it's ready to go, or type their @username the way you'd text a friend. This page explains every part of it in plain language. You don't need to know anything about crypto.

Just say it“Send Josh 20 for pizza.” Monyx fills it in, you tap to confirm.
Split anythingDinner, rent, tickets. Everyone gets a request for their share.
Watch the marketsPro mode adds live crypto prices and prediction markets from Gemini.

01The basics

Monyx is built on Solana, a public payment network that anyone can use, like the internet is for email. When you pay someone, the money moves from your account to theirs over that network in about a second.

You never see any of that. Monyx turns long account numbers into usernames, shows everything in dollars, and handles the technical parts for you.

The one thing to remember: your dollars live in your account, which only you control. Monyx can't move, freeze or spend them. (Invest and Predict only show prices from Gemini. Monyx never holds coins or bets for you there.) That's also why your recovery key matters so much.

02Talk to pay

Tap Just say it on the home screen (or the mic on the pay screen) and say what you want, the way you'd tell a friend. Monyx works out who, how much and what for, and shows you the payment, ready to send.

SayWhat happens
“Send Josh 20 for pizza”A $20 payment to Josh with the note 🍕 Pizza, waiting for your tap
“Request 15 from Andy for coffee”A $15 request to Andy, waiting for your tap
“Split 60 with Josh and Andy for dinner”The split screen with both of them added
“What's my balance?”Monyx tells you, out loud
“Who paid me?”Your last few payments, read out loud

Voice never sends money by itself. It only fills in the payment. You always see the person's name, photo and the amount, and nothing moves until you tap Pay. If Monyx mishears, you lose a tap, not your money.

Hearing when you get paid

With Read payments aloud on (in the Me tab), Monyx says it when money arrives: “Josh sent you 14 dollars for pizza.” It also tells you when someone asks you for money. Turn off Monyx talks back if you'd rather read than listen.

This works within a few seconds even if the money came from outside Monyx (another wallet, an exchange). While the app is open, Monyx checks the network every few seconds for dollars arriving at your account and tells you straight away.

How it works

Your browser's own speech recognition turns your voice into text, and Monyx's replies are read out by your device's built-in voice. Monyx never records or stores audio. In browsers without speech recognition (like Firefox), type the same sentence and it works the same way.

03Your account

Signing up

Signing up takes four steps: your name, an @username, a lock (Face ID, fingerprint or a password), and saving your recovery key. There's no email.

What actually happens

When you sign up, your browser creates a private key: a long secret code that works like the only key to a safe. It's made on your device, and only a locked copy ever leaves it (see below). From it comes your account number, which is public, like a mailbox address. Monyx saves just three things: your name, your username, and which account number belongs to it.

Your recovery key

Your recovery key is that private key, written out so you can save it. It's your last way back in: if you can't use a passkey, a password or another device, it still works.

Monyx can't reset it. There's no "forgot password" because we never had your password or your key. If you lose your recovery key and every other way to sign in, the account can't be recovered. And anyone who has it can spend your balance, so never share it, not even with someone claiming to be Monyx.

Keeping your key safe

Signup asks you to lock your key. You can change it later in the Me tab.

  • Lock with a passkey. Your key is stored encrypted on your device and only opens with Face ID, your fingerprint or your device PIN. Even someone who gets into your browser's storage sees only scrambled data. Monyx also keeps a copy locked with the same passkey, so if your passkey syncs (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager) it signs you in on your other devices too.
  • Lock with a password. For devices without passkeys. Your key is encrypted with the password on your device. Leave "Also use it to sign in on other devices" on and Monyx keeps a copy locked with it, so your @username and password work anywhere. The password itself never leaves your device, and wrong guesses are limited.
  • Save to your password manager. Monyx can save your recovery key to iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or 1Password, filed under your @username.

Logging in

To log in, your device proves it holds the key by signing a short message that names the site, plus a one-time code from Monyx and the time. Monyx checks the signature, and that the code is fresh and unused, then lets you in. You stay signed in on that device until you tap Log out: opening the wallet goes straight to your account, and Monyx only asks for Face ID, your fingerprint or your password at the moment you send money or view your recovery key. A copied signature can't be reused, and one collected by a look-alike site won't work here. The key itself is never sent anywhere.

On a new device, open the wallet, tap I already have an account and choose how:

  • Passkey: Face ID or fingerprint, if your passkey has synced to this device.
  • Password: your @username and sign-in password.
  • A code from your other device: tap Use a code from my other device, then approve it on a device where you're signed in. Check that the emojis match on both screens before you approve.
  • Recovery key: paste it. This always works.

If you already use a Phantom crypto wallet, you can log in with that instead.

04Paying & requesting

Tap the $ button in the middle of the tab bar, type an amount, choose who it's for, and add a note if you want.

→
The networkSolana moves the money in about a second
→
They have itIt's in their account, ready to spend

The money goes straight from your account to theirs. Monyx only adds one thing: it pays the network fee (a fraction of a cent) by adding its signature to the payment you already signed. It can't change who the payment goes to or how much. That's why there's no fee for you, and why you never need any crypto to send dollars.

Paying someone who isn't on Monyx

Tap Send with a link, to anyone (or just say "send Mom 20"). Monyx moves the money into a one-off account whose key is only inside the link, and you text the link to them. They open it, make a free account, and the money is theirs. Until then, you can cancel it from Me → Pay links and get it back. Treat the link like cash: anyone who has it can claim it.

Requests

Tap Request instead of Pay and they'll see it on their home screen with Pay and Decline buttons. When the money arrives, Monyx marks the request paid on its own and tells you. It works even if they paid from another wallet, as long as it's the same amount. You can cancel a request you sent any time.

Payments are final

Like handing someone cash, a payment can't be reversed once it's sent. If you pay the wrong person, ask them to send it back.

05Splitting a bill

On the pay screen, type the total and tap Split a bill with friends. Add people and choose whether to include yourself, and Monyx works out each share.

Say dinner was $60 for three people including you. Each share is $20, so your two friends each get a request for $20 and you collect $40.

06Adding money

New accounts get $5 in test dollars to try things out. After that, tap Add money on the home screen:

  • Add free test dollars: one tap, $2 a day, straight from Monyx.
  • Get paid by a friend: share your pay link or QR code.
  • Need more for testing? The Add money sheet shows your account number with a copy button and a link to Circle's free faucet. Choose Solana Devnet, paste your account number, and test USDC arrives in your balance (Circle limits how often you can ask). Or ask a friend on Monyx to pay you.

07Invest & Predict

Both tabs are part of Pro mode (switch modes in the Me tab). Monyx only shows the data. Every Trade on Gemini button opens Gemini, where you use your own Gemini account.

Invest: crypto prices

Live prices for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and other big coins from Gemini's public price feed, with today's change and a 7-day chart. Prices refresh every 30 seconds.

If you bought test coins in Monyx before Invest moved to Gemini, they're listed under Your test coins, and you can sell them back to cash any time.

Predict: prediction markets

Yes-or-no markets from Gemini on real events in sports, politics, the economy, crypto and culture. Each outcome shows a percent, which is its current market price and works like the crowd's estimate of how likely it is. It has its own tab because it's closer to betting than investing. Gemini Predictions is for US users only.

Monyx only shows the markets and prices. It never places bets, buys coins, holds positions or touches money for them. Everything about a trade is between you and Gemini.

08Safety & privacy

What Monyx knows

  • Your name, username and account number
  • Payment requests you send and receive
  • Your crypto trades and the coins it holds for you
  • Payments it helped send (it pays the fee), and payments it saw arrive for you
  • When you use voice: the sentence you said, long enough to understand it. Recordings aren't kept. Full details are on the privacy page.

What Monyx never has

  • Your recovery key or private key
  • Your password. Only a locked copy of your key is kept, and only if you turn on sign-in from other devices.
  • An email or bank details. We never ask for them.

Payments are public. Solana is a public network, so anyone can look up an account number and see its payments, amounts and notes. They won't see your name unless they already know which account is yours. Keep anything private out of payment notes.

When something goes wrong

If a payment fails, Monyx tells you in plain words: "Nothing was sent," or "We couldn't confirm it yet, check your activity." If a crypto purchase goes through but doesn't get recorded straight away, Monyx remembers it and finishes it the next time you open the app.

09Test mode

Monyx is in test mode right now, which is why you'll see a "Test mode" tag. The app, the payments and the network are all real software doing real work, but they run on Solana's test network with test dollars (test USDC). Test dollars have no value: you can't cash them out, and you can't lose real money. It's a safe way to try everything.

Your balance is in USDC, a digital dollar, so $1 is always $1. It doesn't move with crypto prices.

10Pro mode

Pro adds three tools to the same app, with the same look: Invest and Predict get their own tabs, and Trace is under Me → Trace an account (on a laptop it's in the sidebar). The badge in the top right says Pro Mode; tap it, or the Monyx logo, to go back to Home.

  • Invest: live crypto prices from Gemini.
  • Predict: live prediction markets from Gemini.
  • Trace: paste an account number or type an @username to see where its money came from, two hops back, checked against a list of flagged accounts. You get High, Medium or Normal. It's a risk estimate only, not legal or financial advice. The same check runs on its own before you pay someone.

11Questions

Does Monyx charge fees?

No. The Solana network charges a fee of a fraction of a cent per payment, and Monyx pays it for you. You never pay a fee or need any crypto to send dollars.

I lost my recovery key. Can you help?

If you're still logged in on a device, open the Me tab, tap Recovery key and save it now. If you're not, you can still sign in with your passkey, your sign-in password or a code from another device, and then save it. If you've lost all of those, the account can't be recovered. Monyx only holds a locked copy it can't open.

Someone asked for my recovery key. Should I give it?

Never. Anyone with it can empty your account. Monyx will never ask for it.

Can I undo a payment?

No, payments are final. Ask the person to send it back.

Why did my balance change when I didn't do anything?

Your dollar balance only changes when money comes in or goes out. Check Activity: a friend may have paid you. Coins in Invest do change value as prices move.

Can an AI assistant spend my money?

No. If you connect Grok Bot (Me → AI assistant), it can see your activity, ask friends for money and draft payments. Every draft waits on your home screen until you tap Pay. You set a weekly limit and can remove it any time. How it works

Can I use Monyx on more than one device?

Yes. On the new device, open the wallet, tap “I already have an account” and use your passkey, your @username and password, a code from your other device, or your recovery key.

My friend isn't on Monyx. Can I still pay them?

Yes. Send them money with a link: they open it, sign up in under a minute, and it's theirs. You can cancel it until they claim it. If they already have a Solana account, you can also paste their account number.

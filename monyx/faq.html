FAQ

Beginner questions, answered plainly.

If you're new to wallets, start here. If your question isn't on the list, it's fair to assume we haven't written it down yet.

Monyx is a beginner-friendly wallet we built to add a check before you send. You can pay with an @username or a QR. Before anything goes out, we write the payment in plain English and tag the destination High, Medium, or Normal risk.

The public demo runs on Solana devnet. Balances there are play money. You can practice the send flow without moving real funds.

Devnet is a practice version of the Solana network. It behaves a lot like the real thing, but the tokens are not worth real money. That's why we can let people try Monyx without asking them to risk a balance.

Open the wallet, say who to pay with an @username, a QR, or a pasted address, then read the preview. Confirm only if the sentence and the risk tier look right. There's a longer walkthrough on the How it works page.

They're risk tiers on the destination, written in everyday words. High means pause and take a closer look. Medium means slow down. Normal means nothing unusual jumped out.

01 / Why

Why we built this

Payment apps make you find the person, type the amount and type the note. Crypto apps add more chores on top: a wallet, an address, a gas token, a seed phrase. Each one is a place a new user gives up.

Stablecoins on Solana already fix the hard part. A dollar stays a dollar, and it settles in about a second for a fraction of a cent. What was missing was an app that felt like texting a friend.

Usernames instead of addresses. Dollars instead of tokens. We pay the gas. And you can just say what you want.

02 / Features

What it actually does

  1. 01

    Say who to pay

    Hold the mic and say "pay Maya 20 for pizza" or "split 60 with Sam and Leo". ElevenLabs hears it, Monyx fills in the payment, and reads it back. Typing works too.

  2. 02

    See it before it goes

    Every payment stops on one screen: their face, their @username, the amount and the note. Nothing leaves until you tap Pay.

  3. 03

    Pay anyone with a link

    No account on the other end? Send a link. The money waits in its own escrow until they open it and claim it, or you take it back.

  4. 04

    An assistant that can't spend

    Connect Grok Bot and it can ask friends for rent and draft payments. Drafts sit under "Waiting for your OK". It has no way to send on its own.

We also split bills, lock your key behind a passkey on your device, tell you the moment you're paid through QuickNode Streams, and have an Invest tab. Monyx Pro traces how money has moved around any address.

Monyx runs on Solana devnet with test USDC. Balances are test dollars with no real value.

03 / Next

What happens after test mode

Going live means real USDC on Solana mainnet, a licensed partner for moving dollars to and from a bank, identity checks where the law requires them, and moving Invest to real on-chain assets or a regulated broker.

Until then, nothing in Monyx has real value. Your key stays on your device, and Monyx can't move your money.

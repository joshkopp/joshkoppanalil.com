Features

What it actually does.

Eight things, starting with the send screen, where we spent most of our time. Everything here works in the demo today.

01

Pay by @username or QR.

Type @maya, or scan a code. You can still paste a long address if you want to. Most of the time you won't.

Send to

@maya

02

A plain-English preview.

Before you confirm, we write out what you're about to do. "You're sending two dollars to Maya." If something looks off, you see it here, not buried in a hex string.

03

An address safety check.

We look at the destination and give it a risk tier you can actually use: High, Medium, or Normal. These labels are here to help you pause. They are not legal or financial advice.

04

Fake tokens can’t fake your balance.

Scammers send people tokens with a copied name and logo, hoping they look official and get clicked. Monyx only counts one thing: Circle’s USDC. Anything else that lands in your account, even if it’s called “USDC,” never shows up in your balance.

Your balance counts

Circle USDC only.

Look-alike tokens are ignored, so they can’t pose as money you have.

05

Just say who to pay.

Hold the mic and say “send Maya twenty for pizza” or “split sixty with Sam and Leo.” Monyx fills in the payment, reads it back, and shows the same preview and risk check. If something is unclear, it asks instead of guessing. Typing works too.

“Split sixty with Sam and Leo.”

06

Dollars in, dollars out. No fees.

Your balance is USDC, a digital dollar that stays worth a dollar. Sending on Solana needs a tiny network fee in a token called SOL. Monyx pays it for you, so you never have to buy SOL or know it exists. Payments arrive in about a second.

07

Pay links, for people without Monyx.

Make a link, text it, done. The money waits in its own account until they open the link and claim it. If they never do, cancel it from your Me tab and it comes back. Everyone also has a personal link and QR code friends can use to pay them.

What they see

Josh sent you $20.00.

Tap to claim. New to Monyx? Making an account takes about ten seconds.

08

An assistant that can’t spend your money.

Connect Grok Bot with a key from your Me tab. It can see your activity, ask friends to pay you, and draft payments with a weekly limit you set. Drafts wait on your home screen for your OK. There is no way for the key to send money by itself.

Also in the wallet

The smaller things.

  • Request money. Ask someone for a set amount. They get it on their home screen with a Pay button.
  • Split a bill. Type or say the total and the names. Everyone gets a request for their share.
  • Live alerts. A note pops up the moment money lands in your account.
  • Passkey lock. Lock your account key behind Face ID, Touch ID, or your phone’s passkey, or save it to your password manager.
  • Invest. Buy and sell Bitcoin, Ether, Solana and more at real prices with your test dollars, to learn how it feels.
  • Monyx Pro. A tool that traces how money has moved in and out of any Solana address.

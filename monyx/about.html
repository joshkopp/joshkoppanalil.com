About

The two of us, and how this started.

We're Josh Koppanalil and Andrew Hu. We built Monyx after getting scammed ourselves while learning crypto.

How this started

We used to think crypto wasn't for us.

We thought crypto was only for really technical people. When we finally jumped in and tried it, we got scammed a lot. Fake tokens, bad links, look-alike addresses. Our wallets never warned us, and once the money was gone there was no getting it back.

So we built the wallet we wish we'd had: one that checks who you're paying before anything leaves, and explains it in plain English.

The demo still runs on Solana devnet. We'd like it on other chains later, but that's the honest version for now.

About

The two of us, and how this started.

We're Josh Koppanalil and Andrew Hu. We built Monyx at the UBC 2026 hackathon because a friend almost paid the wrong person.

How this started

We got into crypto the usual student way.

A little late, a little lost, and suddenly responsible for money in apps that assumed we already knew the rules. Then we watched friends almost mess it up. Look-alike addresses. Tokens that weren't what they said. A group chat that made a send feel urgent.

A friend of ours almost paid the wrong person because two addresses looked the same at both ends. There's no undo on that, which still sits with us. So we built a wallet that looks first.

The demo still runs on Solana devnet. We'd like it on other chains later, but that's the honest version for now.

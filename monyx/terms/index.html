The ground rules

Last updated November 17, 2026

Monyx is a student project in test mode. These terms are written in plain language; by using Monyx you agree to them.

Test mode, test dollars

Monyx runs on Solana devnet, a test network. Dollars in Monyx are test USDC and coins in Invest are test entries. None of it has real value and none of it can be exchanged for real money. Don't send real funds to a Monyx account number.

Your account and your key

  • Your account is controlled by a recovery key that only you have. Anyone with it can take everything in your account. Monyx will never ask for it.
  • If you lose your recovery key and your browser data, the account can't be recovered. There is no reset.
  • You're responsible for what's done with your key and any AI assistant key you create.

Who holds what

  • Your dollar balance is held in your own account on the network. Monyx can't move, freeze or spend it. Monyx pays the network fee by adding its signature to payments you've already signed. It can't change who they go to or how much.
  • Coins in Invest are different. In test mode, Monyx keeps them in its own ledger. When you buy, your dollars go to Monyx; when you sell, Monyx pays dollars back. That part works like a regular brokerage account, and you rely on Monyx for it.
  • Pay links hold money in a one-off account whose key is in the link. Anyone who has the link can claim it, so share it like cash. You can cancel an unclaimed link from the device that made it.

Payments are final

Once a payment is sent it can't be reversed, by you or by us. Check the name, photo and amount on the confirmation screen. Voice and AI assistants only fill in payments; you always tap to send.

Fair use

Don't use Monyx to break the law, harass anyone, impersonate someone, or attack or overload the service. We limit test dollars, payments and requests to keep things fair, and we may remove usernames or accounts that misuse the service.

No warranty

Monyx is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. It may have bugs, go offline, or change at any time, including resetting test balances. To the extent the law allows, the people who built it aren't liable for any loss from using it.

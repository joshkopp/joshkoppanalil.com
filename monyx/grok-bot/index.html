Grok Bot

A money assistant that can't run off with your money

Connect Grok Bot to Monyx and it can chase rent, split dinner, recap your week and draft payments. It can't send anything: every payment it drafts waits for your tap in Monyx.

What it can do

Ask itWhat happens
"Ask Maya and Jordan for their share of the $1,800 rent on the 1st"A routine sends each of them a $600 request every month
"Dinner was $84 with Maya, Jordan and Sam"Three $21 requests, one per friend
"I owe Maya for the concert tickets, 42.50"A $42.50 draft appears in Monyx under "Waiting for your OK"
"How did I do this week?"A summary of who paid you, who you paid, and what's still open
"Who hasn't paid me back?"Your open requests, oldest first, with an offer to nudge

Set it up (2 minutes)

  1. In Monyx, open Me → AI assistant, give it a name and a weekly limit, and tap Create key. Copy the key; you'll only see it once.
  2. Open the Monyx template in Grok Bot and tap Add to Grok Bot. To build your own instead, copy the template (instructions, routines and approval rules) and add the skill file below.
  3. When Grok Bot asks for the Monyx key credential, paste the key.
  4. Say "what's my Monyx balance?" to check it works. Then try "ask Maya for $5 for coffee".

Why it's safe

  • Read, ask and draft only. An assistant key can't send, withdraw or see your recovery key. The server has no way to move your money, even if it wanted to.
  • Weekly limit. You choose how much it can draft per week. Drafts over the limit are refused.
  • You approve every payment with the same confirmation screen as any other payment, labelled "Drafted by your assistant".
  • Remove it any time from Me → AI assistant. The key stops working immediately.

For developers

The same API works with any assistant that can make HTTPS calls. Base URL:

https://nvfxdzxdlizznrknmunl.supabase.co/functions/v1/monyx-pay

Send Authorization: Bearer mx_…. Endpoints: GET /agent/me, GET /agent/activity, GET /agent/people?q=, GET|POST /agent/requests, GET|POST /agent/drafts. The skill file documents each one.

curl -X POST $BASE/agent/drafts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MONYX_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"to":"@maya","usd":42.5,"note":"🎟️ Concert tickets","reason":"You said you owe Maya"}'

Safety

A second look, in plain English.

The risk check is a pause button, not a verdict. Here's what it does, what the three tiers mean, and what it isn't.

Please read this first

Informational only.

Risk labels on Monyx are informational. They are not legal advice, financial advice, or a guarantee that a payment is safe. You still decide whether to send.

The public demo runs on Solana devnet. Balances there are play money. Don't treat this site, or the demo, as a production wallet for real funds.

In short

A label can help you slow down. It cannot promise an outcome.

How the check works

We look at the destination before you confirm.

When you're about to send, we inspect the address or account you're paying and write a short preview of the payment. Next to that sentence, we show a risk tier: High, Medium, or Normal.

The idea is simple. Look-alike addresses (sometimes called address poisoning) can show up in a history so you paste the wrong one. Tokens can copy a name or a logo. People get rushed. The check is our attempt to put a plain-language pause in front of all of that.

It will not catch everything. A "Normal" label does not mean "this is definitely the right person." It means nothing obvious jumped out in the checks we run today.

The checks we run today

  • High The account number starts and ends like someone in your history, but the middle is different. That's address poisoning.
  • High The @username is one letter off from someone you know, and you've never paid it.
  • High The address belongs to a program or to the USDC token itself, not to a person. Money sent there can get stuck.
  • Medium The account isn't on Monyx, so there's no name or photo to check against.
  • Medium The Monyx account was made in the last day.
  • Medium It's more than half your balance, to someone you've never paid.
  • Normal You've paid them before, or it's simply your first payment to a real Monyx account with nothing else odd.

On High, the Pay button stays off until you tick "I checked this with them." The check runs on your device, from what the app already knows. Nothing extra is sent anywhere.

What the tiers mean

Three words you can use.

High risk

Pause here.

Something about this destination deserves a closer look. Don't confirm just because the screen is in a hurry. Re-check the username, the QR, or the address with the person you're paying.

Medium risk

Worth a second look.

Not a hard stop. Maybe a token looks like a copy, or something else is off but not clear-cut. Slow down before you confirm.

Normal risk

Nothing unusual jumped out.

Still read the preview. You're the one sending it. Normal is not a green light to skip the sentence.

How it works

Four steps, then you decide.

We tried to keep the send flow short. The point is the pause in the middle, not a longer form.

  1. 1

    Open the wallet

    The live demo is at monyx.pro. Make an account with a name and a username, and you get free test dollars to practice with. It runs on Solana devnet, so none of it is real money.

  2. 2

    Say who to pay

    Type an @username, scan a QR, paste an address, or just say it: “pay Maya twenty for pizza.” Most of the time a name or a code is enough.

  3. 3

    Read the preview

    We write the payment in a regular sentence and tag the destination High, Medium, or Normal risk. If something looks off, this is where you catch it.

  4. 4

    Confirm only if it looks right

    You're still the one sending it. If the sentence or the tier makes you hesitate, don't confirm. There's no undo after it leaves.

That's the send screen.

For what the risk tiers mean, and what they are not, see Safety. Or just try the demo.

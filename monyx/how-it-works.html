How it works

From hello to paid in four steps.

The whole thing takes under a minute the first time. The only slow part is on purpose: a pause to read what you're about to send.

  1. About 10 seconds

    1

    Open the wallet

    Pick a name and a username. That's the whole signup. Monyx makes your wallet right on your device and drops in free test dollars so you can practice. It runs on Solana devnet, so none of it is real money.

    Create your account

    Your nameJosh Koppanalil
    Username@josh
    Create account
    $5.00 in test dollars added

  2. About 5 seconds

    2

    Say who to pay

    Tap the $ and punch in an amount, then pick a person by @username or QR code. Or skip the typing and say it: "pay Maya twenty for pizza." A pasted address works too, if that's what you have.

    $20
    123456789
    RequestPay
    "Pay Maya twenty for pizza"

  3. The pause

    3

    Read the preview

    Before anything leaves, the payment is written out as a sentence and the destination gets a tier: High, Medium, or Normal. If the account looks like someone you know but isn't, or the username is one letter off, this is where it shows up.

    Preview

    You're sending 20 dollars to Maya Chen (@maya) for "pizza".

    Normal risk

    You've paid Maya before.

    High risk

    @mayah is one letter off from @maya.

  4. About a second

    4

    Confirm, and it's there

    Only tap Pay if the sentence and the tier look right. On High, the button stays off until you tick that you checked. Once it goes, it lands in about a second, with no fee. There's no undo, which is why the pause comes first.

Behind the scenes

What you don't have to think about.

Your key stays with you.

The key that controls your account is made and kept on your device. Lock it behind a passkey or save it to your password manager. We never see it.

We pay the network fee.

Every Solana payment costs a fraction of a cent in SOL. Monyx covers it, so your $20 arrives as $20 and you never have to buy SOL.

Dollars that stay dollars.

Your balance is USDC, a digital dollar. It doesn't jump around like other crypto, so $20 today is $20 tomorrow.

A receipt anyone can check.

Every payment is recorded on Solana. Tap "View receipt" after you pay and you can see it on a public explorer.

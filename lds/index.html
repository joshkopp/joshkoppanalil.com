    How to read a structure

    Bonds

    Each line is one shared pair of electrons. Two lines make a double bond, three a triple.

    Lone pairs

    Pairs of dots belong to one atom only. They push bonds closer together and bend the shape.

    Formal charge

    Valence electrons minus lone electrons minus bonds. A circled sign marks any atom that isn't zero.

    Brackets

    An ion is wrapped in brackets with its overall charge outside, top right.

    Shapes come from VSEPR theory. For molecules with more than one central atom, the readout describes the most connected heavy atom. The 3D model spreads electron regions apart with a simple repulsion model, where lone pairs push harder than bonds, so angles are close to real values but not measured ones.

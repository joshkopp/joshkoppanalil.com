Green lines are shared pairs (bonds). Loose dots are lone pairs. Small badges show formal charge. Geometry is predicted with VSEPR theory; for molecules with several centers it describes the most-connected atom. In 3D, glowing lobes are lone pairs and every bond angle comes from VSEPR, so the model is idealized rather than measured.

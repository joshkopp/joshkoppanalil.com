// How to read it

The legend

Bonds

Each green line is one shared pair (2 electrons). Two lines = double bond.

Lone pairs

Loose dot pairs belong to one atom only. They push bonds closer together.

Formal charge

FC = valence − lone electrons − bonds. Badges show any atom that isn't 0.

Brackets

Ions get brackets with their overall charge. Blue electrons were transferred.

Shapes come from VSEPR theory; for molecules with several centers the panel describes the most connected heavy atom. The 3D model relaxes electron domains with a repulsion model (lone pairs push harder than bonds), so its angles are close to, but not exactly, measured values.

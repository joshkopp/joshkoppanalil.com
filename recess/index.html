How to play

🔦 Hide & Seek — Westbrook Middle School

  • One random player is the seeker and gets locked in the principal's office for 20 seconds.
  • Everyone else hides. Walk up to a locker, cabinet, closet, desk, bookshelf, curtain, box, bed, couch or wall panel and press E. Press E again to come out.
  • Risky spots are quick to get into but your shoes might show. Safe spots take the seeker longer to search. Vents need you to crouch. Some walls aren't walls.
  • The seeker presses E to search a spot or to catch someone in the open. Found players spectate.
  • Hiders win if anyone is still hidden when time runs out. The seeker wins by finding everyone.
  • Voice chat is proximity-based, so the seeker can hear you talking. Stay quiet.

⚡ Tag — Funtopia Complex

  • One player starts as IT, glowing red and 12% faster.
  • IT tags someone with E or a click when they're close. The tagged player becomes IT and is frozen for 2 seconds, and they can't tag the person who just tagged them right back.
  • Sprint with Shift (watch your stamina), cut through rooms, take the stairs in the atriums, and use the maintenance tunnels underneath.
  • Whoever spent the least time as IT after 4 minutes wins.

🧟 Infection — Funtopia Complex

  • One or two players start infected (green). Tag a survivor and they turn into a zombie too.
  • Survivors win if anyone is still standing when the 3-minute timer runs out. The infection wins if it gets everyone.
  • Grab glowing power-ups: ⚡ speed, 🦘 super jump, 🛡️ shield (can't be tagged), 👻 cloak. Step on the glowing jump pads to bounce way up over whoever is chasing you.

🎮 On a school iPad / Chromebook

  • No mouse needed: WASD to move and the arrow keys to look. You can also drag on the screen to look and tap to do the action.
  • Set Graphics to Low in Settings if it stutters.

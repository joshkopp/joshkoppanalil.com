// How it works

From pixels to a guess

Four steps, about 50,000 multiplications, under a few milliseconds.

01

Clean up

Your drawing is cropped, shrunk to fit a 20×20 box, and centered by its center of mass inside 28×28. That's exactly how the MNIST training digits were prepared.

02

Find strokes

8 small 5×5 filters slide over the image. Each one learned to light up for a pattern: a vertical edge, a diagonal, a stroke end.

03

Combine shapes

16 more filters combine those strokes into loops, corners and curves. Max-pooling halves the size each time, so position matters less.

04

Vote

A dense layer of 64 neurons turns the shapes into evidence, and softmax turns the evidence into ten probabilities.
